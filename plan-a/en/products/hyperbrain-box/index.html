Introducing HyperBrain Box

As we enter the era of physical AI, embodied intelligence is moving from isolated, controlled demonstrations to long-horizon tasks in real-world environments. HyperBrain Box is built for this transition.

HyperBrain Box features a configurable high-performance compute module delivering up to 2070 TFLOPS (FP4) of AI compute, with an integrated, independent 340 Wh semi-solid-state battery. A quick-release structure and extensive interfaces support deployment and expansion across multiple robot form factors.

It provides a dedicated brain hub for real-world robot deployments: HyperBrain RSI handles reasoning, planning and orchestration, while HyperBrain Box provides the hardware execution foundation that enables robots to keep completing tasks on site.

CHAPTER 01

From a single pick to a complete task

Imagine asking a robot to complete a job: put away scattered tools, then deliver the required parts to a workstation.

This task involves recognition, movement, grasping and placement. If someone passes by, a tool moves, or the first grasp fails, the robot must reassess the situation and adjust its next action.

A successful grasp is still a long way from independently organizing a home, working outdoors or performing assembly on a production line.

Large models are adept at interpreting abstract instructions. Turning information into safe, reliable and sustained physical operations still requires a practical answer: how can greater computing power be combined with rapid integration and efficient power delivery?

HyperBrain Box brings mounting, connectivity, power and computing together in a unified platform, reducing fragmented hardware and software configuration so development teams can focus on model integration and task validation.

CHAPTER 02

Why HyperBrain Box

Multi-platform compatibility for real-world deployment

Different tasks require different robot form factors, with diverse mounting and peripheral configurations. The quick-release design of HyperBrain Box supports installation and removal in minutes without modifying the robot body. Extensive I/O interfaces and automotive-grade connectivity provide room to expand for different application requirements.

It also supports sustained full-power operation at an ambient temperature of 45°C without throttling. Protection against splashes, dust and other on-site challenges helps ensure continued operation.

Installation and removal in minutesExtensive I/OSustained full power at 45°C

More performance without compromising robot runtime

HyperBrain Box includes a 340 Wh semi-solid-state battery that provides approximately two hours of independent operation. The computing platform receives stable power without drawing on the robot’s own power source, reducing its dependence on the overall power system. It supports operation while charging, so it can keep running when connected to a charger. A 24 V / 60 W power output also supplies common peripherals, further simplifying power deployment across the robot.

For repeated daily use, HyperBrain Box retains at least 80% battery health after 400 charge-discharge cycles, supporting long-term operation with greater usability and durability.

Operates while charging24V / 60W peripheral power output

Powerful AI compute for autonomous on-device intelligence

HyperBrain Box uses a high-performance compute module offering up to 2070 TFLOPS (FP4) of AI compute and up to 128GB of unified memory. Multiple compute configurations support on-device large-model inference, perception processing and task planning.
