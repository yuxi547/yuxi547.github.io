Introducing HyperBrain Box

As we enter the era of physical AI, embodied intelligence is moving from isolated, controlled demonstrations to long-horizon tasks in real-world environments. HyperBrain Box is built for this transition.

HyperBrain Box features a configurable high-performance compute module delivering up to 2070 TFLOPS (FP4) of AI compute, with an integrated, independent 340Wh semi-solid-state battery. A quick-release structure and extensive interfaces support deployment and expansion across multiple robot form factors.

It provides a dedicated brain hub for real-world robot deployments: HyperBrain RSI handles reasoning, planning and orchestration, while HyperBrain Box provides the hardware foundation for sustained on-site task execution.

01 / BEYOND A SINGLE ACTION

From a single pick to a complete task

Imagine asking a robot to complete a job: put away scattered tools, then deliver the required parts to a workstation.

This task involves recognition, movement, grasping and placement. If someone passes by, a tool moves, or the first grasp fails, the robot must reassess the situation and adjust its next action.

A successful grasp is still a long way from independently organizing a home, working outdoors or performing assembly on a production line.

Large models are adept at interpreting abstract instructions. Turning that information into safe, reliable and sustained physical operations still requires a practical answer: how can greater computing power be combined with rapid integration and efficient power delivery?

HyperBrain Box brings mounting, connectivity, power and computing together in one platform. It reduces fragmented hardware and software configuration so development teams can focus on model integration and task validation.

02 / WHY HYPERBRAIN BOX

Why HyperBrain Box

Multi-platform compatibility for real-world deployment

Different tasks call for different robot form factors, mounting arrangements and peripherals. On compatible platforms, HyperBrain Box's quick-release mount enables installation and removal in minutes without modifying the robot's structure. Extensive I/O and automotive-grade connectivity provide room for different peripherals and task configurations.

It also supports sustained full-power operation without throttling at an ambient temperature of 45°C. Protective design features address on-site challenges such as splashes and dust to support continuous operation.

Installation and removal in minutesExtensive I/OSustained full power at 45°C

More compute, without drawing on the robot's battery

HyperBrain Box has a built-in 340Wh semi-solid-state battery for approximately two hours of independent operation. It powers the computing platform without drawing energy from the robot's own battery, reducing reliance on the robot's power system. It can operate while charging, and its 24V / 60W power output can supply common peripherals to simplify power configuration.

Designed for repeated daily use, HyperBrain Box can retain at least 80% battery state of health after 400 charge-discharge cycles, supporting long-term operation and durability.

Operates while charging24V / 60W peripheral power output

Powerful AI compute for on-device intelligence

HyperBrain Box features a high-performance compute module with up to 2070 TFLOPS (FP4) of AI compute and up to 128GB of unified memory. Multiple configurations support on-device large-model inference, perception processing and task planning.
